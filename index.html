During Your Dryer Vent Service

  1. Airflow Testing

    Airflow can be checked to help evaluate dryer vent performance before and/or after cleaning when appropriate.

  2. Dryer Vent System Inspection

    Accessible portions of the dryer vent system are inspected for visible problems, restrictions, loose connections, damaged components, or other concerns.

  3. Dryer Booster Fan Inspection

    If the dryer vent system has a booster fan, accessible components can be inspected for visible problems such as loose connections, deteriorated hoses, improper positioning, or a fan that does not appear to be operating.

  4. Dryer Vent Service Certificate

    Service documentation or a service certificate can be provided when applicable. Dryer vent service certificates are available for homeowners, HOAs, condo associations, apartment communities and assisted-living facilities as documentation of completed preventative maintenance.

Warning signs

Signs You Need Service

Small warning signs can turn into expensive problems. Mister Clean My Vent helps homeowners restore proper airflow, drainage, and exterior appearance before damage gets worse.

Clothes Take Too Long to Dry

Your dryer may need more than one cycle, clothes may feel unusually hot, or the laundry room may become warm and humid.

Burning Smell or Excessive Heat

A hot dryer, burning odor, or overheating appliance may indicate restricted airflow caused by lint buildup.

Water Overflowing From Gutters

Water spilling over the sides during rain can mean the gutters or downspouts are clogged.

Plants or Heavy Debris in Gutters

Visible leaves, dirt, plants, or organic buildup can prevent water from draining properly.

Green or Black Growth on Siding

Algae, mildew, mold, and organic staining can make siding look dirty and continue spreading if left untreated.

Dark Streaks and Exterior Discoloration

Streaking on siding, trim, gutters, soffits, or other exterior surfaces may indicate that the home needs soft wash cleaning.

Why Homeowners Call Us

Homeowners call us when airflow is restricted, gutters stop draining, or exterior surfaces begin showing dirt and organic growth. We identify the problem and recommend the right service for the property.

Our process

Our Process

Every service starts with an inspection and ends with a final walkthrough. Our process is designed to protect your property, solve the problem, and leave the work area clean.

Dryer Vent Cleaning

  1. Inspection

    We inspect the dryer connection, transition hose, vent route, and exterior termination.

  2. Professional Cleaning

    Lint and debris are removed using professional compressed-air tools and specialized vent-cleaning equipment.

  3. Airflow Verification

    Downspouts are checked for blockages, and water flow is tested where appropriate.

    Removed debris is collected, the work area is cleaned, and any visible gutter concerns are explained.

Dryer Vent Cleaning FAQ

How often should my dryer vent be cleaned?

Most dryer vents should be professionally cleaned at least once a year. Homes that do frequent laundry, have pets, or have longer vent runs may need more frequent cleaning.

What are the signs of a clogged dryer vent?

Common signs include clothes taking longer to dry, the dryer becoming unusually hot, a burning smell, excess lint, or weak airflow from the exterior vent.

Can a clogged dryer vent cause a fire?

Lint is highly combustible. Excessive lint buildup and restricted airflow can increase heat inside the dryer vent system and create a potential fire hazard.

How do you clean dryer vents?

We use professional equipment designed to loosen and remove lint and debris from the vent line while restoring airflow.

Do you remove bird nests from dryer vents?

Yes. We provide bird nest removal and can replace damaged exterior vent covers or install a protective bird guard when appropriate.

Do you replace dryer vent hoses?

Yes. We can replace damaged, crushed, excessively long, or poorly connected dryer transition hoses.

How long does dryer vent cleaning take?

Most standard cleanings take approximately 30-60 minutes. More difficult vent systems or heavy blockages may take longer.

Gutter Cleaning FAQ

How often should gutters be cleaned?

Most homes should have their gutters cleaned once or twice a year. Homes surrounded by trees may require more frequent service.

Why is gutter cleaning important?

Clogged gutters can cause overflowing water, damage to fascia and soffits, foundation concerns, and water buildup around the home.

Do you clean the downspouts too?

Yes. We check downspout flow and clear accessible blockages as part of our gutter cleaning process.

Do you clean underground downspout drains?

Underground downspout drain clearing may be available as an additional service. We can inspect the line and determine whether it can be cleared with our equipment.

Do you install gutter guards?

Yes. We offer gutter guard installation to help reduce leaves and larger debris from entering the gutter system.

Will gutter guards eliminate gutter cleaning completely?

No gutter guard system is completely maintenance-free. However, a properly installed system can significantly reduce debris buildup and cleaning frequency.

House Washing FAQ

What is house washing?

House washing is an exterior cleaning service designed to remove dirt, algae, mold, mildew, and organic buildup from siding and other appropriate exterior surfaces.

Do you pressure wash siding?

We use a cleaning method appropriate for the surface. Siding is generally cleaned using a lower-pressure application and rinse rather than aggressive high pressure.

Is house washing safe for vinyl siding?

When performed correctly with appropriate cleaning methods and pressure, house washing is suitable for most vinyl siding.

How often should I have my house washed?

Many homeowners schedule house washing every one to two years, depending on trees, shade, moisture, and exterior buildup.

Will house washing remove green algae?

Yes. Green organic buildup and algae are common reasons homeowners schedule a house wash.

Driveway, Sidewalk & Patio Cleaning FAQ

What surfaces do you clean?

We clean many concrete driveways, sidewalks, walkways, and patios. Surface condition is inspected before cleaning.

Can you remove black stains and algae from concrete?

We can significantly improve many dirt, algae, mold, and organic stains. Some permanent stains, rust, oil, or deeply embedded discoloration may require specialized treatment and may not be completely removable.

Will pressure washing damage my concrete?

We use appropriate equipment and cleaning techniques based on the surface and its condition. Older, damaged, or deteriorating concrete is inspected before service.

Can you clean my patio?

Yes. We provide patio cleaning for appropriate concrete and other approved exterior surfaces.

Do you clean driveways and sidewalks at the same time?

Yes. Driveway, sidewalk, walkway, and patio cleaning can be combined into one exterior cleaning service.

How long does exterior surface cleaning take?

The time depends on the size, condition, and amount of buildup. Smaller areas may take a few hours, while larger exterior cleaning projects may take longer.

Do I need to be home during the service?

Not always. As long as we have access to the agreed service areas and water source, many exterior cleaning services can be completed without the homeowner being present.
