Warning signs

Call when your dryer starts telling on itself.

Loads need two or more cycles to dry
The laundry room gets hot or humid
You notice a burning or dusty smell
The exterior vent flap barely moves or appears blocked
Lint collects behind or around the dryer
The dryer shuts off before the load is dry

Common problems

Restricted vents are not always obvious from inside.

Lint, nesting material, damaged covers, and blocked exterior vents can all reduce airflow. We inspect accessible areas and point out what needs attention.

Bird Nest at Exterior Vent

Nesting material can block airflow and keep the exterior flap from opening.

Nesting Inside the Vent Line

Debris inside the duct can trap lint and create a serious restriction.

Lint-Packed Vent Guard

Exterior guards can collect lint and may need cleaning or replacement.

Clogged Exterior Cover

Blocked covers can hold heat and moisture in the vent run.

FAQ

Dryer vent cleaning questions.

How often should my dryer vent be cleaned?

At least once a year is recommended for most homes. Homes with larger families, heavy laundry use, pets, or longer vent runs may need service more often.

What are signs my dryer vent may be clogged?

Common signs include clothes taking two or more cycles to dry, the laundry room feeling hot or humid, a burning or dusty smell, lint collecting behind the dryer, or the exterior vent flap barely moving.

Do you check airflow before and after cleaning?

Yes. We perform airflow checks to help verify that the dryer vent is moving air better after service.

Do you service apartments, condos, and rental properties?

Yes. We offer dryer vent cleaning for homes, apartments, condos, rental properties, laundry rooms, and small multi-unit properties.

Can you replace dryer vent covers or add a bird cage?

Yes. If the exterior vent cover is damaged, missing, or needs protection from birds or debris, we can offer vent cover or bird cage installation when appropriate.
