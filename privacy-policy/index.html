Mister Clean My Vent LLC

Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, store, and share information when you use our website, request service, communicate with us, submit an advertising lead form, or make a payment.

Effective date: November 18, 2026

Mister Clean My Vent LLC ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to handling customer information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains our practices concerning personal information collected through our website, service and estimate requests, communications, payment processes, and Facebook or Instagram lead advertisements.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • Contact information, such as your name, phone number, email address, and preferred contact method.
  • Property and service information, such as a residential or commercial address, city, state, ZIP code, requested service, property details, scheduling preferences, and information you include in a message.
  • Customer and service records, such as estimates, appointments, work orders, service history, technician notes, invoices, payment status, and communications.
  • Service-related files that you provide or that are created for a job, such as signed work orders, receipts, and before-and-after or job documentation photos.
  • Lead information submitted through Facebook or Instagram advertisements and Meta Instant Forms, including the fields you choose to submit.
  • Transaction information associated with a payment, such as the amount, date, payment status, and customer contact details made available to us by the payment processor. Full payment-card details are entered on and processed by Stripe, not on our website.
  • Technical and usage information processed by our hosting and analytics providers, such as IP address, browser and device information, referring page, pages viewed, interactions, timestamps, and approximate location derived from an IP address.

2. How We Collect Information

We may collect information through:

  • Our website appointment and contact form.
  • Facebook and Instagram advertisements or Meta Instant Forms.
  • Phone calls, text messages, emails, and social-media messages.
  • Estimates, appointments, service visits, work orders, invoices, and follow-up communications.
  • Our Stripe-hosted payment page.
  • Automated website hosting, security, and analytics technologies.

3. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Respond to inquiries and service or estimate requests.
  • Provide pricing, schedule and confirm appointments, and communicate about requested services.
  • Perform services and maintain customer, work-order, payment, and service-history records.
  • Prepare invoices, process payments, document completed work, and provide customer support.
  • Follow up on leads and customer requests, subject to applicable communication and marketing-consent requirements.
  • Operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve our website and business processes.
  • Prevent fraud or misuse and comply with applicable legal, tax, accounting, and recordkeeping obligations.

Submitting an estimate or service request does not obligate you to purchase a service.

4. Website Forms and Business Records

Our website appointment and estimate-request forms are processed by Netlify Forms. A submission may include your name, phone number, email address, property address, city, ZIP code, requested service, preferred service timing, and optional message. Dedicated advertising landing pages may also record the form source, submission date and time, referring page, landing-page URL, campaign parameters, and advertising click identifiers such as a Meta click ID when those values are available. Netlify stores verified form submissions in the site's protected Netlify account. Form notifications may also be delivered to an authorized business email address when that notification is enabled in Netlify.

Information needed to manage leads, customers, appointments, and completed services may also be entered into our private customer-management system. That system uses Google Sheets for structured customer and service records, Google Drive for authorized service documents and photos, and Google Calendar for appointment scheduling. Access to the customer-management system is restricted to authenticated business users.

5. Cookies, Analytics, and Technical Data

Our public website uses Google Analytics to help us understand website traffic and usage. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar identifiers and may process information such as browser and device details, pages viewed, interactions, referring information, timestamps, and general geographic information. On the dedicated gutter cleaning advertising pages, Google Analytics follows the same regional privacy choice described below for the Meta Pixel: United States visitors may opt out, while visitors outside the United States or whose region cannot be determined must first accept optional tracking. Google handles this information under its own privacy practices.

Netlify hosts the website and may process server logs, IP addresses, request details, and related technical information to deliver, protect, and operate the site. A secure session cookie is also used for authenticated access to our private customer-management area; it is not used as an advertising cookie.

Our gutter cleaning advertising pages use the Meta Pixel to measure advertising results. For visitors in the United States, the Pixel may load under an opt-out model unless the visitor previously declined advertising tracking or the browser sends a supported Global Privacy Control signal. Visitors outside the United States, and visitors whose region cannot be determined, are asked to accept or decline before the Pixel loads. The Pixel records a PageView when tracking is permitted and a Lead event only after the website confirms a successful gutter cleaning quote submission. We do not include the name, phone number, email address, property address, or other text entered in the form in those event parameters.

Meta may receive technical and advertising information such as the page URL, event time, browser or device details, IP address, and Meta cookie or click identifiers. Meta may use that information to measure advertisements, attribute conversions, and provide advertising services under its own Privacy Policy. The advertising Pixel does not load when a visitor declines advertising tracking, when prior consent is required but has not been provided, or when the browser sends a supported Global Privacy Control signal.

6. Facebook and Instagram Lead Forms

We advertise services through Facebook and Instagram. When you submit information through an advertisement or Meta Instant Form, Meta processes the submission on its platform and makes the information you chose to provide available to us. We use that information to respond to your request, discuss your property's needs, provide an estimate, and schedule service when requested.

We do not treat an estimate request as consent to receive unrelated promotional messages. Where required, we obtain separate consent before sending marketing communications. Meta handles information according to its own privacy policy and platform settings.

7. Payments

Our website links to a Stripe-hosted payment page. Payment credentials and full card details are submitted directly to and processed by Stripe rather than being collected by our website. Stripe may provide us with transaction details needed to confirm and administer a payment, such as customer contact information, the amount, date, payment method type, and payment status. Stripe's handling of information is governed by its own privacy policy and terms.

8. How We Share Information

We do not sell or rent customer contact information. We may share information only as reasonably necessary with service providers that help us operate the business, including:

  • Netlify for website hosting and form processing.
  • Google services for analytics, email and communications, customer and service records, file storage, and appointment scheduling.
  • Stripe for payment processing.
  • Meta when you interact with our Facebook or Instagram advertisements, lead forms, pages, messages, or—when permitted by the privacy controls described in this policy—the Meta Pixel on a dedicated advertising page.
  • Other professional or operational providers when needed to deliver requested services, administer the business, or meet legal obligations.

We may also disclose information when required by law, legal process, or a valid government request, or when reasonably necessary to protect customers, our business, or our legal rights.

9. Third-Party Websites and Services

Our website links to services operated by third parties, including Stripe, Google Maps and Google Reviews, Facebook, Instagram, and Nextdoor. When you follow an external link or use a third-party form or payment page, that service may collect information under its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review the applicable third-party policy.

10. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to requests, provide services, maintain customer and business records, process payments, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and satisfy legal, tax, accounting, insurance, and recordkeeping obligations. Retention periods vary based on the type of information and the reason it is maintained. When information is no longer reasonably needed, we may delete or securely dispose of it, subject to applicable requirements.

11. Information Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures intended to protect information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or misuse. These measures include restricted access to business systems and authenticated access to the private customer-management area. No method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

12. Your Privacy Choices

You may contact us to ask about, correct, or request deletion of personal information we maintain, subject to applicable law and legitimate business, security, tax, accounting, and recordkeeping needs. You may also ask us to stop nonessential marketing communications.

On the dedicated advertising pages that use Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel, United States visitors can opt out at any time using the “Privacy Choices” link. Visitors in regions where prior consent is required are shown compact Accept and Decline choices before optional tracking loads. Previously saved choices are honored, and a supported browser Global Privacy Control signal prevents optional tracking from loading. These choices do not affect the quote form. Additional choices relating to cookies or third-party platforms may be available through your browser, device, Google, Meta, or Stripe settings.

13. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for adults and property decision-makers. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review and address the request.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, technology, business practices, or legal requirements change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
